Delete your Dino's Adventure account

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This page explains how to delete your account in Dino's Adventure: Platformer Game (the web game at dino.spanion.in and its Android and iPhone apps, by Ankit Kumar Pandit), and what is deleted.

### Delete it in the game

If you signed in with Google or with an email address:

1. Open Dino's Adventure and tap the **Settings** (gear) button on the home screen.
2. Scroll down to **Delete account** and tap **Delete**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

### Ask us to delete it

If you can't use the game any more, or you play without signing in (the game then gives you an anonymous account, shown on the leaderboards with a generated name such as "Swift Raptor 42"), email [panditankit1995@gmail.com](mailto:panditankit1995@gmail.com) with the subject "Delete my account" and tell us:

- the email address you sign in with, or
- for an anonymous account, the name shown next to your scores on the leaderboards.

We may ask you a question to check the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

### What is deleted

- Your sign-in account: your email address, display name and, for Google sign-in, your Google account identifier.
- Your saved progress: levels, stars, records, streaks, coins and the looks you earned.
- Your leaderboard entries, daily and endless.
- Your public leaderboard profile (your worn looks and the day you last played).
- Your daily reminders: your devices' push tokens and your time zone.
- Your Premium status.
- Your likes on shared worlds, and the records the game keeps to check runs and limit how often things can be sent.

### What is kept

- **Worlds you shared** stay public so other players can keep playing them, but they are credited to "Anonymous" and no longer linked to you. To have a world removed too, ask us at the address above.
- **Anonymous usage statistics** sent to Google Analytics carry no name, email address or account identifier, so they can't be linked to your account.
- Totals that include your plays (such as a world's play count) stay as numbers, with nothing that identifies you.
- Our server's logs of the deletion itself are kept for up to 30 days, for security.

Progress saved only on a device stays in that browser or app until you clear it: in the game, **Settings → Reset progress**.
